## Speed is a feeling

Users don't measure milliseconds, they feel them. Optimistic updates, skeleton screens and instant feedback on hover often matter more than shaving 50ms off a request.

## A tiny checklist

- Respond to input within 100ms, even if the work finishes later
- Animate with `transform` and `opacity` only
- Prefetch what the user is likely to click next

```ts
const next = await prefetch("/projects");
```
